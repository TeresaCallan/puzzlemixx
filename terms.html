7. Share your win

After you win a complete PEACE or The Mental Workout, tap Share your win and send the ready-made message from any app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Messages, Facebook, X or email). For example: “Here is my complete PEACE: Cardio [link to the song on YouTube] Join the game! @PuzzleMixx www.puzzlemixx.com”.

  • +5 points are added as soon as you choose an app and share, or copy the message. We don’t check or see what you post.
  • Each win can be shared for points once, and only for wins in the current round. If you win the same complete PEACE or Mental Workout again after pressing Start over, that new win can be shared for points too. You can share as often as you like, but without more points.
  • Please only send the message to people who are happy to receive it, and follow the rules of the app you use.

8. Fair play

Play the puzzles yourself, or with family members playing alongside you on your account. We may remove points from, or disqualify, anyone who uses more than one account, uses software, scripts or other people to earn points, interferes with the website, or gives false information (including about their age or country). If we do, we will tell you why by email.

9. How winners are decided

  • When a round ends, the three eligible players with the most points in that round (more than 0) win 1st, 2nd and 3rd place.
  • Ties are decided by sudden death. If players are tied for a prize place, the first of them to gain points in the next round takes the higher place, the next takes the place after, and so on until every prize place is filled. Tied players are told on the leaderboard.
  • Backstop: if a tie is still not settled 30 days after the round ended, the tied player who reached their final score first during that round takes the higher place, using the times recorded by our system.
  • Places are worked out automatically from the points recorded when the round closes, subject to section 8 and the test-round note at the top of this page.

  • Winners are told on the leaderboard the next time they sign in after the round ends, with a “Claim your prize” button in their profile. If a winner has not claimed within 14 days, we will also email the address on their account.
  • To claim, send your full name, postal address, country, email address and, for the 1st prize, your chosen gym, studio or sport association (and a back-up registered charity), within 60 days of the round ending.

  • If, after at least two attempts to contact a winner, we cannot reach them, or a prize is not claimed within 60 days, the prize passes to the next eligible player in that round.
